Caloundra
Halogen Downlights & Smoke Alarm Upgrades

Old halogen downlights and non-compliant smoke alarms are the most common issue we find in Caloundra homes. Most properties built before 2010 still have them — and many owners don't realise the fire risk.

Nambour
Ceramic Fuse Box & Switchboard Upgrades

Nambour is one of the Coast's oldest towns and its housing stock shows it. Ceramic fuse boxes are common — they're a fire hazard with no RCD protection, and banks and insurers are increasingly flagging them at sale.

🏚️ Older Homes · Switchboards

Maroochydore
Commercial Electrical & 3-Phase Power

Maroochydore is the Sunshine Coast's business hub. Fit-outs, switchboard capacity upgrades, 3-phase power installs and commercial compliance work — we handle it all for businesses across the precinct.

🏢 Commercial · 3-Phase Power
View Maroochydore guide →

Buderim
Renovation & Extension Wiring

Buderim's established homes from the 70s, 80s and 90s are being heavily renovated. Old wiring that can't handle modern loads, non-compliant bathroom zones, and outdated sub-circuits are what we find most often.
