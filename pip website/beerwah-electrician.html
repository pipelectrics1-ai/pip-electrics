The Beerwah Reality

Why Acreage & Shed Homes
in Beerwah Need a Rural Sparky

Beerwah sits in semi-rural country under the Glass House Mountains — acreage blocks, big sheds, workshops, and a growing number of shed-homes and second dwellings. It's a suburb where distance, three-phase and sheds define the electrical work far more than the house itself.

🏚️

Powering Sheds Across Distance

The shed is the heart of a Beerwah block, and getting reliable power to it — often a hundred metres or more from the house — is a real job. Undersized cable causes voltage drop, dim lights and tripping under load.

  • Correct cable sizing for long runs to sheds and workshops
  • Dedicated circuits for welders, compressors and machinery
  • Sub-boards so the shed has its own protected supply
  • Three-phase where machinery or pumps demand it
  • Weatherproof, rural-rated installation throughout
🏠

Shed-Homes & Dual Living

More Beerwah owners are living in sheds or adding a second dwelling. These need to be wired and metered like a proper home — compliant, safe and separately protected — not treated as an afterthought.

  • Full habitable wiring for shed-homes and second dwellings
  • Sub-metering and separate switchboards for dual living
  • Smoke alarms and safety switches to residential standard
  • Correct circuit design for kitchens, hot water and A/C
  • Compliance certificates for occupancy and rental
