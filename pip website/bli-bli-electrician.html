What We See Most on Bli Bli Acreage Properties

After working on properties throughout Bli Bli and along the Maroochy River corridor, these are the two electrical issues that come up again and again.

🏚️

Shed & Workshop Sub-Boards

It's extremely common to find Bli Bli sheds that are powered entirely by a long extension lead run from the house — or have a decades-old board with no safety switch protection and wiring that hasn't been touched since it was installed. This is a serious safety risk.


We install proper dedicated sub-boards for sheds and workshops: correct cable sizing, weatherproof enclosures, RCD protection on all circuits, and the right number of GPOs for how the space is actually used. Done once, done properly.

⚠️

Single-Wire Earth (SWE) Rural Supply

Older acreage properties in Bli Bli and surrounding rural areas were often connected to the grid via a single-wire earth (SWE) system — a legacy supply method that uses the ground itself as the return conductor. This is no longer considered safe and must be upgraded to a standard two-wire supply.


SWE systems can cause stray voltages, are incompatible with modern safety switches, and can pose a real electrocution risk — particularly in wet conditions. If your property still has SWE, we can assess the work required and manage the Energex upgrade process.

Rural Switchboard & Changeover Switch Work

Changeover switches are common on rural acreage properties with generators or solar systems — they allow you to safely switch between grid power and a backup supply without backfeeding into the network.

We install and certify changeover switches, generator inlets, and full rural switchboard upgrades across Bli Bli and the surrounding Maroochy River area. All work is fully compliant and certificated.

If your meter box is old, undersized, or not properly protected — or if you're adding a shed, bore pump or irrigation system — a switchboard upgrade is usually the starting point. We'll assess what's needed and give you a clear, written quote.
