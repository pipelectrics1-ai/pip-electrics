What Matters in New Builds

The Decisions That Define Your
Home for the Next 30 Years

Bokarina Beach is one of the fastest-growing new developments on the Sunshine Coast. Every buyer here is building brand new — which means every electrical decision is made once, and getting them right at rough-in stage saves thousands down the track.

🔧
Getting the Pre-Wire Right
In a new build, decisions made at rough-in stage define what's possible for the life of the home. EV provision, solar capacity, data points — these are all far easier to do now than to retrofit later. Once the walls are closed:
  • Adding a data point means cutting into finished plasterboard and repainting
  • Running an EV circuit after the slab means surface conduit through a finished garage
  • Upgrading to a solar-ready board after installation adds cost and downtime
  • Relocating power points in a tiled kitchen area is expensive and disruptive
The right time is now. A well-planned pre-wire with your family's lifestyle in mind means the home is ready for everything — today, and for decades to come.
☀️
Solar-Ready from Day One
Bokarina's new homes are perfect candidates for solar — new roofs, optimal orientation, no legacy wiring to work around. A solar-ready switchboard with the right isolators and capacity means adding panels later is simple and cost-effective. Without it:
  • Solar installers may find the board can't accommodate an inverter safely
  • A switchboard upgrade at solar install time adds $800–$1,500+ to your solar quote
  • Undersized mains cable limits how large a solar system you can run
  • Missing isolation points mean extended downtime during any future work
We specify solar-ready capacity and the correct isolators as standard in all Bokarina new home switchboards — so solar is a simple addition when you're ready, not a complex retrofit.
