The Doonan & Eumundi Switchboard Problem

Why Most Hinterland Homes
Can't Go Solar Without an Upgrade

Doonan and Eumundi have some of the best roof areas in the Sunshine Coast hinterland — large acreage homes, north-facing rooflines, and sustainability-minded owners who want to generate and store their own power. The problem is the switchboard.

⚠️
Old Switchboards That Aren't Solar-Ready
Many older homes in Doonan and Eumundi still have ceramic fuse boards or early-generation switchboards that were never designed for solar inverter connections. Without a dedicated solar isolator — a separate AC isolator at the switchboard — your solar installer simply cannot legally connect a system. On top of that, old boards often lack the spare circuit capacity needed for battery circuits, EV charging, and future loads. A solar-ready switchboard upgrade is not optional; it's the foundation. We replace old fuse boards with modern RCD-protected switchboards sized for solar, battery, and EV charging — all in a single visit.
⚠️ Most common reason solar installs are delayed in Doonan & Eumundi
🔋
Battery Storage Retrofit — More Complex Than It Looks
Adding a Tesla Powerwall, Sungrow SBH, or Alpha-ESS battery to an existing solar system is not a plug-and-play exercise. It requires a dedicated battery circuit wired from the switchboard, a gateway or energy management unit installed and configured, careful integration with the existing solar inverter, and — often — a switchboard upgrade to accommodate the new loads. We've wired battery retrofits across the Noosa hinterland and understand what each system needs. Whether you're adding storage to an existing 6.6kW system or building a full solar-battery setup from scratch, we do the electrical side correctly so your installer can commission without issues.
