Yes — smoke alarm installation and compliance is one of the most common jobs we do on the Sunshine Coast. Queensland law now requires interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms in every bedroom, hallway connecting bedrooms, and on every level of the home. All alarms must be hardwired or powered by a 10-year non-removable battery.

This applies to all properties being sold, leased or substantially renovated. We'll assess your existing alarms, advise what's needed, and install compliant alarms across your whole home in a single visit. Call Matt to book.

Your switchboard likely needs upgrading if any of the following apply:

• You still have old ceramic fuses instead of modern circuit breakers
• There are no safety switches (RCDs) on all circuits — or none at all
• Breakers trip frequently, especially when running multiple appliances
• You're planning to add solar panels or a home battery
• You want to install an EV charger or air conditioner but there's no spare capacity
• You've noticed a burning smell or discolouration near the board

We offer free switchboard assessments — call us or read more on our switchboard upgrades page.

No — and this is worth understanding clearly. In Queensland, all electrical wiring work must be carried out by a licensed electrician. This includes installing power points, changing light fittings that require wiring, adding circuits, and any work inside your switchboard. DIY electrical work is illegal under the Electrical Safety Act 2002, carries heavy fines, and — more importantly — can be fatal. It can also void your home insurance and cause issues when you sell your property. If you're unsure whether something needs a licensed electrician, just call us and ask — we're happy to advise.
