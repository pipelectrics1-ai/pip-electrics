What Kawana Homeowners Are Upgrading Most

What Kawana Homeowners
Are Upgrading Most

After working across Kawana Waters, Parreara, Warana and Bokarina, these are the two electrical upgrades Kawana homeowners are requesting most — and both come back to the same root cause: homes built in the 90s and 2000s that were never designed for the way we live and work today.

📡
Poor Home Data Infrastructure
Kawana Waters was largely built between the late 1990s and early 2000s. These homes came with one or two phone points and absolutely no structured data cabling. That was fine when everyone browsed the internet on a single desktop computer. It's a serious problem when you have:
  • Two adults working from home on video calls simultaneously
  • Kids streaming and gaming in separate rooms
  • Smart TVs, home assistants and security cameras all competing for WiFi bandwidth
  • WiFi dead spots in bedrooms furthest from the router
WiFi extenders are a band-aid. A properly installed Cat6 structured cabling system with data points in every room gives you reliable, gigabit-speed wired connections where you need them. We retrofit this into existing Kawana homes with minimal disruption — running cable through roof cavities and wall cavities to put a data point exactly where it needs to be.
💼
Home Office Electrical
Post-COVID, the spare bedroom home office has become a permanent fixture for many Kawana dual-income families. The problem: most Kawana homes have a single standard power point in what is now a full-time workplace. That means:
  • Monitor, desktop, laptop charger, phone charger and desk lamp all running off one double GPO
  • Adaptor and powerboard sprawl under the desk
  • No dedicated circuit — the office shares with the rest of the room or hallway
  • Poor earthing for sensitive workstation equipment
A proper home office setup means: a dedicated 20A circuit, double GPOs at desk height on both sides, USB-C outlets for device charging, a wired Cat6 data point at the desk, and task lighting on a separate circuit. We do the full package — electrical and data — in one visit.
Switchboard and circuit work in Kawana — adding dedicated circuits for home offices, data infrastructure and smart home systems often starts with a switchboard assessment to confirm capacity and safety.
