Commercial Electrical — Maroochydore

Maroochydore Is Growing Fast.
The Electrical Demands Are Too.

Maroochydore is the Sunshine Coast's main commercial centre — Sunshine Plaza, Ocean Street, Horton Parade, and the new CBD development are bringing in new businesses, new tenancies, and new fit-outs constantly. Mixed-use buildings have complex electrical requirements. Rapid development means the grid is under pressure and businesses need to upgrade to stay compliant and operational.

⚡
3-Phase Power & Capacity Upgrades — Businesses Are Outgrowing Single-Phase
It happens constantly in Maroochydore: a cafe installs commercial kitchen equipment, a gym adds new equipment, an office upgrades its A/C — and suddenly the single-phase supply can't keep up. Tripping circuits, brownouts, and compliance issues follow. We assess your current supply, design the upgrade, and install 3-phase power with minimal disruption to your business. We also handle switchboard capacity upgrades when the existing board simply doesn't have the headroom for a growing business.
⚡ Common in Maroochydore's fast-growing commercial precinct
📋
Commercial Compliance — Exit Lighting, RCD Protection & Test-and-Tag
Every commercial tenancy in Maroochydore must have compliant exit and emergency lighting tested to AS/NZS 2293, RCD protection on all power circuits, and — if you have portable appliances — a test-and-tag program. This catches out a lot of businesses during fit-outs or lease renewals. We handle all of it: design, supply, install, test, certify, and provide the paperwork your body corporate or building manager needs. One call, done right.
📋 Required for all commercial tenancies in QLD
Commercial Switchboard & Capacity Upgrades

Maroochydore's rapid development means many existing switchboards are at capacity. We design and install commercial switchboard upgrades — from sub-boards in mixed-use buildings to full 3-phase distribution boards for new tenancies.
