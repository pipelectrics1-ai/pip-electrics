What We See in Mooloolaba

Outdoor & Coastal Electrical —
What We See Most in Mooloolaba

After working across Mooloolaba, Alexandra Headland and the beachside suburbs, these are the electrical problems we find most often — and they're almost always related to salt air or outdoor living upgrades done without the right fittings.

🌊
Weatherproofing & Corrosion
Salt air is relentless. Standard fittings — the same ones you'd install in an inland suburb — fail within 2 to 3 years near the coast. We regularly find:
  • Non-IP-rated outdoor GPOs that have corroded internally and are a shock or fire risk
  • Exterior light fittings that have filled with moisture and failed
  • Switchboard components — terminals, connections, neutral links — showing salt corrosion
  • Corroded cable entry points allowing moisture into junction boxes
The fix: Marine-grade or IP65+ rated fittings and corrosion-resistant componentry throughout any outdoor or exposed installation. Done right, they last. Done with standard hardware, they don't.
🏊
Pool & Entertainment Areas
Mooloolaba has one of the highest densities of residential pools on the Sunshine Coast, and outdoor kitchen and entertainment areas are a feature of almost every property. Australian Standards define strict electrical zones around pools and spas — and many existing installs don't comply.
  • Pool lights with no RCD protection or wired on wrong circuit type
  • Pump circuits not meeting zone distance requirements
  • Outdoor kitchen circuits undersized for the connected loads
  • DIY or builder-grade alfresco installs without correct weatherproofing
Holiday let properties are particularly affected — years of deferred maintenance and multiple owner changeovers often mean safety issues have been overlooked. A safety inspection catches these before they become serious.
Coastal switchboard work — salt air corrosion and moisture ingress are common in Mooloolaba's beachside properties. We identify and replace components before they fail.
