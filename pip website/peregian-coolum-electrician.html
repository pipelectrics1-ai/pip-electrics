The Local A/C Problems We Solve

Two Common A/C Problems
We Fix Every Week Around Here

Peregian Springs and Coolum Beach are two very different suburbs — but both share the same challenge: homes that weren't built or wired for the level of cooling that a hot coastal Queensland climate demands. Here's what we see most often.

🏡
Homes Without Adequate Cooling — Peregian Springs
Peregian Springs is full of family homes built in the 2000s and 2010s — and many of them were built with just one small split system for an entire four or five bedroom house. That might have seemed fine when temperatures were mild, but Queensland summers have gotten hotter and these homes are genuinely uncomfortable without proper whole-home cooling.

We regularly install a full complement of split systems for these homes — living areas, main bedroom, kids' rooms, and sometimes a multi-head unit to reduce the number of outdoor compressors. These are first-time proper A/C installs for families who've been putting up with one inadequate unit for years. If your Peregian Springs home needs its first real cooling system, we'll plan it out properly and install it cleanly.
❄️ Most common request — Peregian Springs families
⚡
Old Undersized A/C Circuits — Coolum Beach
Coolum Beach has a large stock of older beach houses — many of them originally built as weekenders or holiday shacks that are now being renovated and lived in full-time. These older homes often have wiring that simply cannot support modern inverter air conditioning units.

The problem is the existing circuits are too small — the cable gauge and circuit breaker aren't rated for the load of a modern reverse-cycle unit. Installing a new A/C onto an old, undersized circuit is a fire risk and will usually trip the breaker repeatedly anyway. We do the full job: upgrade the wiring and circuit breaker to the correct rating, then install the new unit — so it's safe, compliant, and works properly from day one. No shortcuts, no workarounds.
⚡ Common in older Coolum Beach homes pre-2000

A proper circuit upgrade for a Coolum Beach beach house renovation — new dedicated A/C circuit with correctly rated cabling and breaker, installed before the new split system went in. Clean, safe, and compliant.
