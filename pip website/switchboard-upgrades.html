Why Upgrade Now

Your old switchboard is holding your home back

Ceramic fuse boards from the 60s and 70s weren't designed for modern electrical loads — let alone solar, battery storage, EV chargers, or multiple air conditioners. They're also a fire risk when fuse wire is incorrectly replaced.

A modern switchboard gives you safety switches on every circuit, enough capacity for everything you want to add, and is a requirement before any solar or battery installer can connect their system.

Reasons to Upgrade

Is your switchboard telling you it's time?

Any one of these means you should get a quote.

You have ceramic fuses
Ceramic fuse boards are a fire risk and can't protect your home from modern electrical faults. Fuse wire is too easy to replace incorrectly.
You're getting solar or a battery
Solar and battery installers require an upgraded switchboard with the correct isolation and capacity before they can connect their systems.
Breakers trip frequently
If you're regularly resetting tripped breakers, your switchboard doesn't have the capacity for your current electrical load.
You want to add an EV charger or AC
EV chargers and split systems require dedicated circuits — if there's no room in your existing board, an upgrade is needed first.
Selling your home
Buyers and building inspectors regularly flag old switchboards. An upgrade adds value and removes a common sale condition.
No safety switches on all circuits
QLD regulations now require safety switches on all circuits. Older homes often only have them on power and lighting — not everything.

Common Questions

Switchboard Upgrade FAQ

How long does a switchboard upgrade take?
Most residential switchboard upgrades take 4–6 hours. Power will be off while we swap over the board — usually no more than 2–3 hours. We plan the job to minimise disruption.
Do I need a switchboard upgrade to get solar?
Often, yes. Solar installers require adequate space in your switchboard for the solar isolation switch and inverter circuit breaker. Ceramic fuse boards cannot be used and must be replaced before solar can be connected.
How much does a switchboard upgrade cost?
Pricing depends on your current board, the number of circuits, and what you need to add. We provide a fixed price quote after assessing your existing setup — call or fill in the form below for a free quote.
Can you upgrade my switchboard and install an EV charger on the same day?
Yes. If you need both, we'll quote the switchboard upgrade and EV charger installation together and do it all in one visit. This saves money on call-out costs and minimises disruption.
Do I need to be home during the upgrade?
We'll need access to the switchboard location and a point of contact on the day. You don't need to stay home throughout, but we ask that someone be reachable by phone in case we have any questions.
