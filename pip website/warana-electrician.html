The Warana Reality

Why Beachside Homes
in Warana Are Due an Update

Warana is a laid-back beachside strip along the Kawana coast — a mix of original beach houses and renovated homes, most within a short stroll of the sand. That lifestyle is the draw, but it means tired lighting, ageing boards and coastal wear are common across the suburb.

💡

Tired, Inefficient Lighting

Plenty of Warana homes still run old halogen and fluorescent lighting — hot, power-hungry and dim by modern standards. A full LED upgrade brightens the home, cuts running costs and removes the fire risk of hot old fittings.

  • Old halogens running hot in the ceiling
  • Flickering, ageing fluorescent battens
  • High lighting running costs compared to LED
  • Poor light levels in kitchens and living areas
  • Insulation-safe LED retrofits done properly
🧂

Coastal Wear & Ageing Boards

Sea air is relentless on beachside homes, corroding outdoor fittings and switchboard parts, while many Warana boards are simply old. Together they're a safety issue worth getting on top of.

  • Corroded outdoor outlets and light fittings
  • Ageing switchboards without full RCD protection
  • Weatherproof, coastal-rated fittings needed outdoors
  • Nuisance tripping as loads grow
  • Modern board and safety switches as the safe baseline
A full LED lighting upgrade in a Warana home — brighter, cooler and cheaper to run than the old halogens it replaced.
