The Wurtulla Lighting Problem

Why Wurtulla Homes Need
an LED Upgrade

Wurtulla is a quiet, well-established suburb — and most of its housing was built through the 1990s and early 2000s. That era came with lighting that made sense at the time, but is now costing homeowners money every single day.

🔆

Old Fluorescent & Incandescent Lighting

Wurtulla's 90s–2000s homes use far more power than they need to. Fluorescent tubes in kitchens and garages flicker as they age and give off a harsh, cold light. Incandescent globes in living areas burn hot and consume five to ten times the energy of a comparable LED. LED replacements use up to 80% less energy and last 10× longer. Colour temperature makes a huge difference to how a home feels — switching to warm-white LED throughout transforms the atmosphere of every room.

  • Fluorescent batten lights in kitchens, garages and laundries
  • Incandescent globes in living areas and bedrooms
  • Flat, unpleasant lighting throughout with no warmth or depth
  • No dimming capability on any circuits
  • High power draw adding to quarterly electricity bills
🔦

No Exterior Sensor Lighting

Most Wurtulla homes have a single porch light — and nothing else outside. Driveways, side entries and back yards are completely dark after sunset. Sensor-activated LED floodlights on driveways and entry points deter intruders and are immediately practical for arriving home at night, taking bins out, or moving between the house and garage safely. It's one of the most cost-effective security upgrades available to a homeowner.

  • Driveways and carports completely unlit after dark
  • Entry paths with no motion detection or automatic activation
  • Side gates and back yards invisible at night
  • Intruder deterrence dramatically reduced without sensor lighting
  • Practical inconvenience — fumbling for keys in the dark
Switchboard and circuit work is often part of a full LED upgrade — ensuring circuits can support modern dimmers and new lighting loads.
