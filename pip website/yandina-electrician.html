Frequent & Long Outages in Yandina
Yandina's position in the hinterland means storm damage to the network is common during the wet season — and restoration takes longer than coastal suburbs. Energex restoration crews prioritise high-density areas first, which means rural and hinterland properties like those in and around Yandina can be without power for 6, 12, or even 24+ hours after a major storm event.

A properly installed transfer switch and standby generator means your home or business stays powered regardless of how long the grid is down — fridge, lights, internet, medical equipment, all of it. We've installed these systems right across the Yandina, Eudlo, Bli Bli and Maroochy River areas and we understand exactly what the hinterland demands.
⛈️ Hinterland outages can last 6–24+ hours
⚠️
Generator Wiring Done Properly
Many Yandina homeowners already own a portable generator — but running it through extension leads plugged into your power points is dangerous and illegal. Without a proper changeover switch, your generator can back-feed into the Energex network while it's being worked on, putting linemen at serious risk of electrocution.

A licensed changeover switch physically isolates your home from the grid before connecting generator power. It's not optional — it's the only safe and legal way to use a generator connected to your switchboard. We install manual and automatic changeover switches for all generator types, fully compliant with AS/NZS 3000 and Queensland network operator requirements.
⚠️ Back-feed is illegal and life-threatening for linemen
